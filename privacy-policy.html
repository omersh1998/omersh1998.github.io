Privacy Policy

Evolution Race · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Evolution Race (the "Game"), made by Tomato Games ("we", "us"), collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The Game does not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, gems, upgrades, settings such as sound and vibration) is saved on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data, unless you use cloud save (section 3).

2. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not run our own servers and do not collect personal information ourselves. The third-party services below, which the Game uses, may collect information as described in their own policies.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising). The Game shows ads: short ad breaks between runs, and "rewarded" ads you choose to watch for an in-game bonus. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show, measure and (if you agree) personalise ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where else required, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings ("Privacy").
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (game analytics). The Game sends anonymous usage events, such as runs started and finished, sections cleared, upgrades bought, tutorial completion, ads watched and purchases, with an app instance ID, the advertising ID, device and app information and an approximate location derived from the IP address. We use this only in aggregate to improve the Game. The data is kept for 2 months. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases). Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive a confirmation and an order ID so the purchase can be delivered and restored. Google Payments Privacy Notice.
  • Google Play Games Services (sign-in and cloud save). If you use Google Play Games, the Game signs you in automatically when it starts; your Play Games player ID and your saved game are then stored in your Google account so progress can be restored on another device. You can decline or sign out in Play Games at any time. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Unity (game engine). The Game is built with Unity, which may collect basic, non-identifying device and performance information. Unity Player Privacy Policy.

4. How information is used

  • to show ads and give you the rewards for watching them;
  • to process and restore in-app purchases;
  • to save and restore your progress (if you use cloud save);
  • to understand, in aggregate, how the Game is played (for example where players get stuck) and improve it;
  • to keep the Game working, fix problems and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: change your consent in the Game's Settings, or reset / opt out of your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).
  • No ads: the "No Ads" purchase removes the ad breaks between runs and gives ad rewards instantly, without ads.
  • Cloud save: you can decline the Play Games sign-in, or sign out of Play Games at any time.
  • Deletion: uninstall the Game to delete local data. To delete cloud-saved data, remove the Game's data from your Google Play Games settings, or contact us.

6. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are handled by the services in section 3; we will help you with any request sent to the contact address below. The legal bases for processing are your consent (personalised ads) and our legitimate interest in providing and improving the Game.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and security

Local data stays on your device until you delete the Game. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies. We use the security measures provided by these services; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
